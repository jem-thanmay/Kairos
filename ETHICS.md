# Kairos — Ethical Guidelines

## 1. Never Diagnose
Kairos uses "signal" not "symptom". "Pattern" not "condition". It never says "this person is depressed." It says "what you're describing shares patterns with depression-stage distress." The distinction matters — one is an observation, the other is a label that can cause harm.

## 2. Always Redirect to Professionals
Every insight Kairos surfaces ends with a pointer toward professional support. Not a robotic hotline number — a contextual suggestion that the level of signal being observed warrants a trained conversation.

## 3. Consent is Explicit
The subject — the person being observed — must know the app exists if observations are being logged about them. Kairos is a support tool for the observer, not a surveillance tool. This is stated clearly in the UI.

## 4. Kairos Can Say "I Don't Know"
When signals are ambiguous, conflicting, or below confidence thresholds, Kairos says so. No false confidence. Uncertainty is displayed honestly rather than hidden behind a number.

## 5. Crisis Protocol is Hardcoded
When signals cross a defined threshold — specific language around self-harm, hopelessness scores above a limit, or explicit crisis indicators — Kairos stops being subtle. It directly surfaces crisis resources regardless of cultural framing, relationship context, or any other factor. This is non-negotiable.

## 6. Privacy by Design
All processing happens locally. No data is sent to external servers. No observations, no text inputs, no model outputs leave the user's device. This is especially critical given the sensitivity of mental health data.

## 7. Cultural Humility
Kairos was designed with awareness that mental health expression varies significantly across cultures. The guidance it generates is calibrated to avoid Western-centric assumptions about how distress is expressed or how help-seeking should look. This is an ongoing area of improvement.

## 8. Not a Replacement
Kairos does not replace therapy, clinical assessment, or human connection. It is a signal-raising tool that helps observers be more present and effective for someone they love. The goal is always to strengthen human connection, not replace it.

---

*These guidelines are living documentation. As Kairos develops, ethical review will be ongoing.*
